Business Recommendations
Encourage customers to switch from month-to-month contracts to yearly plans by offering discounts and rewards.
Promote auto-pay, credit/debit cards, and bank transfer payment methods instead of electronic checks.
Bundle tech support, online security, and device protection services into affordable packages.
Improve customer experience during the first few months because new customers are more churn-prone.
Increase customer engagement through personalized offers and service recommendations.
Provide loyalty benefits to long-term customers to improve retention.
Focus more on high-risk customers with low engagement and fewer subscribed services.
Improve trust and relationship-building strategies with flexible payment users.
